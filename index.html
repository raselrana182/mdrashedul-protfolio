Featured research papers

My doctoral work examines how to safeguard machine learning inputs and reduce privacy risks without unnecessarily sacrificing predictive performance. The papers below describe the approaches and evaluations reported in my published research.

Paper 01 / Gaussian processes + privacy

Privacy-Preserving Gaussian Process Regression with Random Unitary Transformation

Research challenge. Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) provides predictions and uncertainty estimates, but access to model behavior can expose information about training examples. Privacy defenses can also reduce predictive accuracy.

Approach. I investigated Random Unitary Transformation (RUT), using orthogonal transformations of input features to protect representations while retaining geometric relationships important to the GPR kernel when compatible transformations are used.

Evaluation. The study examined predictive performance and privacy risks under encrypted training and testing configurations, including membership inference attack evaluation and comparisons with noise-based protection.

Paper 02 / Federated and distributed AI

Federated Gaussian Process Regression with Orthogonal Feature Encryption and Key-Based Access Control

Research challenge. Federated learning limits direct sharing of raw data, but distributed learning systems can still face inference-time privacy risks and access-control challenges.

Approach. I developed and studied a federated GPR framework combining orthogonal feature transformations with key-based access control, focusing on how compatible and incompatible transformation keys affect predictions and access to protected representations.

Evaluation. Experiments investigated prediction behavior, predictive variance, model utility and privacy implications in distributed settings.

Paper 03 / Selective protection

Selective Feature Encryption for Secure Gaussian Process Regression

Research challenge. Protecting every feature may be unnecessary when privacy needs or application constraints differ across input variables.

Approach. This collaborative work investigated applying transformations selectively to feature subsets in GPR, with the goal of protecting selected information while examining the effects on useful predictions.

Evaluation. The research studied how selective protection affects predictive behavior, extending the broader investigation of privacy-preserving feature transformations.

Research approach

Connecting privacy protection with practical machine learning

My work considers privacy threats, authorized model access, and predictive utility together.

01 · Preserve model utility

Study transformations that retain important geometry for Gaussian Process modeling, under compatible processing conditions.

02 · Evaluate privacy risks

Investigate membership inference and the information an adversary may obtain from model outputs, including uncertainty.

03 · Support distributed settings

Extend privacy-aware methods to federated environments where access rights and cross-party model behavior matter.
