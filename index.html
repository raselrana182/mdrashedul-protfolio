About me

Research at the intersection of AI, privacy, and security.

My work focuses on understanding privacy leakage and security risks in machine learning systems, then developing practical ways to protect information while maintaining model utility.

I completed my Ph.D. in Interdisciplinary Intelligent Systems Engineering at the University of the Ryukyus in September 2026. My doctoral research explored secure Gaussian Process Regression, feature transformations, federated learning, and membership inference risks.

Research

Selected research directions

Core themes from my doctoral research and current research interests.

01 · Privacy protection

Privacy-Preserving Gaussian Process Regression

Developed methods using Random Unitary Transformation to protect data used in Gaussian Process Regression and investigate their effects on predictive utility.

02 · Distributed AI

Secure Federated Gaussian Processes

Developed a federated GPR framework using orthogonal feature encryption and key-based access control in distributed settings.

03 · Privacy evaluation

Membership Inference Risk

Evaluated membership inference attacks and examined how secure feature transformations influence privacy risks and model performance.

04 · Research interests

AI Attacks, Defenses, and Trustworthiness

Interested in model inversion, adversarial machine learning, and privacy-preserving AI for edge and IoT environments.
